# Clip-it
Clip-It makes copying and pasting powerful, secure, and effortless.
- Never lose what’s important. 
- Save, organize, and paste anything — text, links, images, files, and more — instantly across all your devices.
-  Reuse frequently asked questions, product descriptions, pricing details, payment methods, and pickup instructions into reusable snippets. 

#### ✅ Simple & intuitive
Clip-It was designed to be effortless. Just copy — and it’s saved. Your clips are always ready when you need them.

#### 🏷️ Organize with tags
Create custom tags to keep your clips tidy and easy to find. Filter by project, or category — your workspace, your rules.

#### ☁️ Sync with iCloud
All your clips are securely stored and available on every device. Copy on your Mac, paste on your iPhone, and vice versa — continuity that just works.

#### ⌨️ Always ready with the keyboard
Paste your saved clips anywhere using the Clip-It Keyboard extension. Your texts, links, files, and memes are just one tap away in any app.

#### 🚀 Boost your productivity
Reuse common replies, templates, code snippets, or documents in seconds — stop typing the same thing twice.

#### 💬 Sell online & reply in seconds
Perfect for online sellers (Facebook Marketplace, eBay, Craigslist, Etsy, Poshmark) and customer support. Save frequently asked questions, product descriptions, pricing details, payment methods, and pickup instructions into reusable snippets — answer buyers instantly with 1-tap pasting without retyping the same messages over and over.

#### 😂 Centralized Meme & Media Hub
Save memes, reaction GIFs, and images from different apps (Safari, X/Twitter, Reddit, Instagram, Messages) all into one central place, neatly organized and ready to share later in any conversation without cluttering your Photos library.

#### 🔒 Private & secure
Your clips belong only to you. Everything stays encrypted and private, protected inside your iCloud account.

#### 🧭 Privacy-friendly links
Clip-It automatically removes tracking information from links — keeping your data and browsing habits private.

#### 📲 Easy to capture
Create clips from within the app, the share sheet, or simply drag & drop content on iPad — Clip-It adapts to your workflow.

#### 🎁 Shared App Bundle 
Unlock premium features across our entire ecosystem of participating apps (such as iBorder and Unloop) with a single subscription. * Requires an active Apple iCloud account on the device.
